## 6. Invariants and enforcement boundary

**Declarative DB constraints where applicable:** primary/foreign keys; no hard-delete of referenced lines/events/assets; positive factors and receipt quantities; valid item/UOM/condition/tracking shape; retry-key uniqueness scoped to operation/actor context; initial-handover business uniqueness per attempt; active exact-asset reservation uniqueness; opening batch-line uniqueness. Distinct retry UUIDs cannot bypass business identity.

**DB transaction / controlled operation enforcement (not a cross-row CHECK):** lock existing item/location/cohort parent or safely upsert the balance identity before locking; deterministic lock order for source/destination, assets and reservations; compare expected revisions; atomic all-or-nothing event/ledger/projection/audit/outbox; validate transition, actor/domain, aggregate bounds and conservation. RLS/grants must deny direct ledger/balance writes; authorization is enforced at the DB boundary even behind a server façade. DDL/security design remains unapproved.

- `on_hand = good + damaged`; physical buckets never negative.
- Transfer sum per item/base unit/cohort/condition = 0; condition change preserves physical total; no conversion reapplied during transfer/return.
- `eligible_good` excludes expired/unknown/prohibited stock; `available_for_new = max(0, eligible_good - committed)` is allowed **only together with** `shortfall = max(0, committed - eligible_good)` and protected allocations. Do not display a clamp as proof commitments are fulfilled.
- Example good 10, committed 8, damage 3 ⇒ physical good 7, damaged 3, commitment 8, fulfillable 7, shortfall 1, new availability 0. Multiple-request attribution needs explicit audited reallocation; do not silently select a losing request.
- For returnable lines: `handed_net = returned_net + resolved_unrecovered_net + return_due`, all right-side values nonnegative. Over-return/over-offset is rejected or enters controlled correction, never hidden with max.
- Waiver: initial 30, issue 7 ⇒ 23, return 6 ⇒ 29, resolve 1 ⇒ still 29. Late return 1 ⇒ 30, returned 7, resolved net 0, due 0.
- Nonreturnable/in-place lines do not synthesize this return obligation. All request-level mandatory initial return confirmations still apply under Q8.
- Signature and notification delivery each have stock effect 0. Actual event cannot post twice under either same-key retry or distinct-key double click.
- Posted evidence is append-only; a correction cannot rewrite what a signer saw. Catalog deactivation blocks new issue, not authorized return of historically issued material.
- No physical stock is authoritative in two systems at once; opening reconciliation covers balances, assets, in-custody/outstanding facts and evidence in the chosen scope.
